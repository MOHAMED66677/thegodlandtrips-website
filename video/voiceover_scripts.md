# Voiceover scripts: TheGodLandTrips reel (6 languages)

Each video lasts **29.8 seconds** and has 8 scenes. Record one line per scene, starting at the time shown. The lines are short on purpose so they fit at a natural pace. Pause between scenes rather than speeding up.

**Recording tips:** use a quiet room, hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, and record in a single take if you can. The video already has soft music, low enough for your voice to stay clear.

| # | Scene | Time | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0.0 – 3.0 s | Logo + "Marrakech." + promise |
| 2 | Problem | 3.0 – 6.4 s | Two struck-through problems + clock |
| 3 | The site | 6.4 – 9.0 s | Laptop + phone showing the site |
| 4 | Services | 9.0 – 13.6 s | Three service cards flip in |
| 5 | Activities | 13.6 – 18.6 s | "13" counter + carousel of activities |
| 6 | Quote | 18.6 – 22.4 s | Form + WhatsApp message |
| 7 | Languages | 22.4 – 25.4 s | Six phones, one per language |
| 8 | Call to action | 25.4 – 29.8 s | Logo + button + WhatsApp number |

## Adding your voice

In **CapCut** (free, on phone or computer):
1. Import `reel_xx.mp4`.
2. Tap **Audio → Record** (or import your recording).
3. Drag each line so it starts at the time shown above.
4. Lower the video's own sound a little (around 60–70 %) if your voice needs more room.
5. Export in 1080p.

To render the videos again (for example after changing the site or the texts):

```
python capture.py http://localhost:8080      # screenshots of the site in each language
python music.py                              # timeline + music bed
python render.py --lang fr                   # one video (repeat for ar, en, es, de, nl)
```
